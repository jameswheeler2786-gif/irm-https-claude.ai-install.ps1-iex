FAQ

Common questions

Who is Outly for?

Outly works for organisations that want stronger communities — universities, student accommodation, councils, hostels, leisure centres and community organisations — as well as the organisers who run activities and the people who take part. Wherever real-world participation matters, Outly makes it easier to discover and join in.

Is Outly a dating platform?

No. Outly is community discovery infrastructure. Dating events are one use case among many — alongside student life, community activities, sports clubs, travel experiences and local events. The platform helps people discover communities and build meaningful connections, whatever the setting.

How do universities and accommodation providers use Outly?

They use Outly to bring societies, events and activities into one place so students and residents can discover them and take part. It supports belonging and participation from week one, and gives a clear picture of what people are actually engaging with.

Can venues partner without running events themselves?

Yes. Venues can make their space available to organisers on the platform. Engaged attendees show up motivated and drive real on-site spend — so quieter and off-peak slots fill more reliably, without one-off promotions.

How does early access work?

Get in touch and we'll learn about your community and onboard you. Early partners get direct access to the founding team, priority onboarding, and the best terms we'll ever offer.

When is the platform launching?

We're onboarding early partners and running live activities on the platform now. Full public launch is coming soon — partners who join during early access have a significant head start on reach and discovery.
