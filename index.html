FAQ

Common questions

Who is Outly for?

Outly is built for hosts, venues, influencers, and community builders who run in-person dating and social events. If you can fill a room with people who want to meet someone — or just want to connect — Outly gives you the infrastructure to make it commercially viable.

Is this only for dating events?

Dating events are our core — highest-intent category, where our outcome system adds the most value. That said, any social event where people want to genuinely connect works on Outly. Networking nights, mixers, social clubs, niche interest events — the Pulse system works across all of them.

Can matchmakers use Outly to monetise their full client base?

Absolutely. Matchmakers often have large books of contacts who aren't paying subscribers. Outly gives them the infrastructure to run paid events for their entire network — creating a new revenue stream with no additional tech build required.

Can venues partner without running events themselves?

Yes. Venues can partner with Outly to make their space available to hosts on the platform. Dating event attendees are high-spending and motivated — they drive real bar and food spend. Consistent bookings, no one-off promotions needed.

How does early access work?

Apply by getting in touch. We'll get in touch, learn about your events, and onboard you. Early hosts get direct access to the founding team, priority onboarding, and the best terms we'll ever offer.

When is the platform launching?

We're onboarding early hosts and running live events on the platform now. Full public launch is coming soon. Hosts who join during early access have a significant head start on audience reach and discovery.
